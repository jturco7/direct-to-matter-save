Direct-to-Matter Save

Outlook add-in for John T. Turco & Associates, PC, LLO

What it does

The Save to Matter button in Outlook lets you save email attachments directly to the correct Centerbase matter folder in SharePoint — no more downloading, renaming, and dragging files around.

How to use it

  • Open an email with attachments in Outlook (desktop, web, or mobile).
  • Click the Save to Matter button on the ribbon.
  • Search for the matter by number, name, or client.
  • Pick the attachments to save and confirm.

Common issues

  • Button not visible: Restart Outlook. New deployments can take up to 24 hours to propagate to all users.
  • Sign-in loop: Sign out of Outlook, close it completely, reopen, and try again.
  • Matter not found: Confirm the matter exists in Centerbase and has been synced to SharePoint. Older matters may need an index rebuild.
  • Save failed: Check your network connection. If the issue persists, contact IT.
